Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before partnering with us.

How quickly can you respond to an IT emergency?
We aim to respond to all support requests within 24 hours, and for clients on managed support contracts, we prioritise urgent issues for same-day response. Emergency support is available outside normal business hours. Contact us for details.
Do you offer once-off services or only monthly contracts?
Both. We handle once-off jobs like repairs, installations and troubleshooting, as well as recurring monthly support contracts. Many clients start with a once-off project and move to a support package once they see the value.
What areas do you service?
We're based in Vanderbijlpark and serve the entire Vaal Triangle, including Vereeniging, Sasolburg and surrounding communities. For larger projects, we can travel further. Just ask.
Do you offer free consultations or quotes?
Yes. We offer a free initial consultation and assessment. We'll review your current setup, understand your needs, and provide a no-obligation quote tailored to your business.
Can you work with our existing IT systems and suppliers?
Absolutely. We integrate with your existing infrastructure and can work alongside your current suppliers or take over completely. Our goal is to make your IT work for you, not disrupt it.
Do you provide support for both hardware and software?
Yes, we cover the full spectrum. From laptop and desktop repairs, hardware upgrades and networking equipment, to operating systems, software installation, cybersecurity and cloud solutions.
